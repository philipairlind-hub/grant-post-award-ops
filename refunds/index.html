Policy

Refunds & cancellation

A straightforward policy for the one-time 60-day founding pilot.

Before work starts

A full refund is available if the customer cancels before onboarding or other work has started.

After work has begun

After onboarding or other work has materially begun, refund requests will be considered fairly based on the work and deliverables already provided and any undelivered part of the agreed service.

If the service cannot be delivered

If Grant Post-Award Ops cannot deliver the service as agreed, the customer will receive a refund for the undelivered portion.

Cancellation

The £490 founding pilot is a one-time 60-day engagement. There is no automatic renewal. The customer can ask to stop the engagement at any time by email.

How to request a refund or cancellation

Email shopairlind@gmail.com with the organisation name and a brief explanation of the request. A response will be provided by email.
