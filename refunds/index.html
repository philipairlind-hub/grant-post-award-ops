Policy

Refunds & cancellation

Simple: if the setup is not useful to you, you get your money back.

Before work starts

If you cancel before you have sent your data, you receive a full refund.

After delivery

If the setup is not useful, tell me within 60 days of delivery and you receive a full refund of the fee paid. You do not need to justify it, though a sentence on why helps me improve the service.

If I cannot deliver

If I cannot deliver what was agreed, you receive a full refund.

No renewal

Both setups are one-time purchases. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

How to ask

Email shopairlind@gmail.com with your organisation name. Refunds are made through Stripe to the original card; banks usually show them within 5–10 working days.
