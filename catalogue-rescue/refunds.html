Cancellation & refunds

Last updated: 30 September 2026.

The pilot is a fixed-scope professional service. Payment is requested only after the project scope has been confirmed by email.

Before work starts

If you cancel before substantive work has started, contact us promptly. Where no work has begun and no non-recoverable cost has been incurred, we will normally refund the payment in full.

After work starts

Once substantive project work has begun, refunds are assessed according to the work already completed and the agreed scope. If we cannot deliver the agreed service, we will discuss an appropriate remedy, which may include correction, partial refund or full refund depending on the circumstances.

Corrections

The standard pilot includes one correction round for issues that fall within the agreed scope. Requests that materially expand the original scope are handled separately.

How to request a cancellation or refund

Email shopairlind@gmail.com with the project name and reason for the request.

This policy does not limit any mandatory rights that apply under applicable law.
