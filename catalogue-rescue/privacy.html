Privacy

Last updated: 30 September 2026.

We aim to collect only the information needed to discuss, scope, deliver and support a catalogue-cleanup project.

Contact information

If you email us, we receive the information contained in your message and your email address so we can respond and manage the business relationship.

Project files

The pilot is intended for product catalogue data only. Please do not send customer, order, payment-card or other unnecessary personal data. Confidential files should only be shared after the project scope and handling method are agreed.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe may collect billing, payment and tax-related information needed to process the transaction. We do not ask clients to send card details by email.

Retention

Project files are retained only as long as reasonably needed for delivery, corrections, support and legitimate record-keeping. Where practical, raw client files are deleted after the agreed delivery/retention period. Minimal records may be kept where required for accounting, payment, dispute or opt-out purposes.

Marketing and opt-out

If we contact a business and the recipient asks not to be contacted again, we keep only the minimum suppression information needed to respect that request.
