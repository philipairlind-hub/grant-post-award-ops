01 / Service

The boring work, done properly.

01

Clean

Normalise agreed fields, fix obvious formatting inconsistencies and prepare a controlled import candidate without inventing missing business decisions.

02

Check

Separate what can be corrected from what needs client or agency judgement, so uncertainty is visible instead of silently guessed.

03

Document

Return the cleaned file with a source-linked change log, exception list and a staging-import checklist your team can actually use.

04

Hand back

Your agency keeps the client relationship, makes ambiguous commercial decisions and performs or approves the final import.

02 / Scope

One file. One mapping. No scope fog.

Included / £195 pilot
  • One client-supplied CSV
  • Up to 250 simple-product rows
  • One agreed field mapping
  • Catalogue cleanup + QA against agreed source rules
  • Import-ready candidate CSV
  • Source-linked change log + exception list
  • Staging-import checklist
  • One correction round
Not included by default
  • Customer, order or payment data
  • Automatic production-store imports
  • Unlimited product copywriting
  • Variable / highly bespoke catalogue logic
  • Commercial decisions where source data is ambiguous
  • Additional source systems or mappings
03 / Process

No black box.

01

Confirm fit

You send a representative sample or describe the catalogue. We confirm whether the fixed pilot is appropriate.

02

Lock rules

We agree the mapping, source rules, exclusions and what should be escalated instead of guessed.

03

Clean + QA

We process the agreed file, document changes and isolate exceptions that need a decision.

04

Agency approval

Your team reviews the output and performs or approves the final WooCommerce import.
