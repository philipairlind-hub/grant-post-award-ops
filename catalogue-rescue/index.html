The service

Turn repetitive catalogue work into a clean handoff.

Designed for migrations, rebuilds, launches and ongoing WooCommerce support where product data arrives inconsistent, incomplete or simply too time-consuming for senior agency staff.

Catalogue cleanup

Normalise agreed fields, remove obvious formatting inconsistencies and prepare a controlled import candidate from the supplied source file.

QA & exception handling

Document what changed and isolate missing or ambiguous data for a human decision instead of quietly inventing values.

Migration preparation

Reduce the spreadsheet burden before WooCommerce imports, replatforming, store rebuilds and catalogue restructuring.

Process

A simple subcontractor workflow.

No client-facing takeover, no production credentials and no opaque automation. Your agency remains in control of decisions and the final import.

Step 01

Confirm fit & mapping

Send a representative sample or describe the file. We agree the fields, source rules, exclusions and what counts as an exception.

Step 02

Clean, QA & document

We process the agreed CSV and produce the candidate file, change log, exception list and staging checklist.

Step 03

Your team approves

Your agency or client resolves commercial ambiguities and performs or approves the final WooCommerce import.

FAQ

Before you hand over a catalogue.

Do you need access to the WooCommerce store?

No. The standard pilot works from the agreed CSV and returns an import candidate and QA documentation. Your agency performs or approves the final import.

What happens when information is missing?

It is flagged in the exception list. We do not make silent commercial assumptions about prices, categories, product attributes or other business decisions.

Can this be delivered white-label?

Yes. The service is designed as subcontractor support for agencies. Your client relationship stays with you.

What if the catalogue is larger or more complex?

We scope it separately after reviewing a representative sample. The £195 pilot is intentionally limited to simple products and a single agreed mapping.

Have a catalogue that is eating agency time?

Send a short description of the file or a representative sample. We will confirm whether it fits the pilot before any payment is requested.
