Service terms

Last updated: 30 September 2026.

Pilot scope

The standard pilot is £195 and covers one client-supplied CSV containing up to 250 simple-product rows, one agreed field mapping and one correction round, unless a different scope is agreed in writing before payment.

Deliverables

The standard deliverables are a cleaned import-ready candidate CSV, a source-linked change log, an exception or missing-data list and a staging-import checklist.

Client responsibilities

The agency or client must provide usable source data and confirm the agreed mapping and source rules. Ambiguous commercial decisions remain with the agency or client. The agency or client performs or approves the final production import.

Timing

The standard target is delivery within two business days after usable input, mapping and source rules have been confirmed. If the source data is incomplete or materially different from the agreed scope, timing may be paused until the issue is resolved.

Excluded work

The pilot does not include customer/order-data processing, unbounded manual content creation, production-store import, or highly bespoke/variable catalogue logic unless separately agreed.

Accuracy and review

The service is designed to reduce repetitive catalogue work and surface exceptions. The agency remains responsible for reviewing the deliverables, making business decisions and approving the final import.
