A focused service

The work after a grant is approved deserves its own structure.

Important post-award evidence can end up distributed across inboxes, spreadsheets, portals, shared drives and separate systems. That makes it harder to see what is complete, what is due and what needs attention.

Grant Post-Award Ops focuses specifically on the post-award period. It works alongside your existing application process or grant-management software; it is not an application portal.
