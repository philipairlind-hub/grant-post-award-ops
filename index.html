What it is not

A narrow setup, not another grants platform.

It does not replace your application process, grant system or accounts. There is no migration, no integration and no access to your systems.

It never moves money or contacts grantees. It organises the information you supply so you can see what needs doing. It is not legal, audit or regulatory advice. Decisions stay with you and your trustees.

Your data

Send as little as possible. It stays with me and is deleted.

Redacted is fine. Replace recipient names with codes if you prefer. Never send bank details or personal data about grantees' clients or beneficiaries.

Processed locally. Your file is processed with a local script on my own computer. It is not uploaded to AI tools or shared with anyone.

Deleted. Your data is deleted within 30 days of final delivery, or sooner if you ask. If you need a data processing agreement, send yours and I will sign it.
