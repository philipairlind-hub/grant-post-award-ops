Policy

Privacy

This page explains the information used to discuss and provide the Grant Post-Award Ops service.

Information that may be processed

This may include business contact information, correspondence, grant portfolio information supplied by the customer, and service records needed to set up, provide and administer the pilot.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, agree the scope of work, configure the post-award workflow, provide the service, maintain necessary service and payment records, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Sharing and access

Information is not sold. It may be handled through service providers needed for ordinary business administration and delivery, such as email, document, payment or hosting providers. Access is limited to what is reasonably needed to provide and administer the service.

Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for service delivery, business records, dispute handling and applicable legal obligations. Retention needs may vary according to the information and the engagement.

Customer responsibilities

Customers should only provide grant portfolio information they are authorised to share and should avoid sending unnecessary personal or special-category information. Appropriate transfer and access arrangements can be agreed before work starts.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions, or to request access, correction or deletion where applicable, email shopairlind@gmail.com.
