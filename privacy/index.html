Policy

Privacy

Who handles what information, why, and for how long.

Who is responsible

Philip Lind (Grant Post-Award Ops), Denmark. Contact shopairlind@gmail.com for any privacy question or request.

If I emailed you

I only contact organisations whose published work is relevant to grant administration, using a work or organisational address published on your organisation's website or a public register. I use it to ask whether the service is relevant. Reply "no" at any time and I will not contact you again. I keep a minimal record so I do not contact you twice.

Customer grant data

If you buy a setup, you decide what to send, and redacted data is welcome. Your file is processed with a local script on my own computer to build your deliverables. It is not uploaded to AI tools, sold or shared. It is deleted within 30 days of final delivery unless you ask me to keep it for a refresh. If you need a data processing agreement, send yours and I will sign it.

Services I use

Gmail for email, Stripe for payments and invoices, and GitHub Pages to host this website. This website uses no analytics or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your information, or object to its use, by email. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) or, in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.
