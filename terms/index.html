Service terms

Terms

These terms apply to the post-award control setup and the administrator setup, sold to business customers.

Who provides the service

Grant Post-Award Ops is run by Philip Lind, an independent operator based in Denmark. Contact: shopairlind@gmail.com. It is a business-to-business administrative support service for grant-making organisations and professional administrators.

Post-award control setup — £290

For up to 60 active grants: a post-award control workbook (Excel) built from the grant list or export you supply, a one-page board exception summary (PDF) and a list of data questions. The first version is delivered within 2 working days of receiving your data and the final version within 7 days. One round of corrections and one refresh from an updated export within 60 days of delivery are included, with email support for 60 days. The workbook models up to three payment tranches per grant.

Administrator setup — £490

For professional administrators: the same deliverables for up to 5 client foundations or portfolios (up to 150 active grants in total), with a separate board summary per client, one combined view, one refresh within 60 days and email support for 60 days.

What is not included

Application or intake processes, migration of your systems, integrations, access to your systems, contacting grantees, making or approving payments, and legal, accounting, audit or regulatory advice. Anything else needs a separate written agreement.

Your responsibilities

Supply the information needed and confirm you are authorised to share it. Review the outputs before relying on them. All grant-making, payment and governance decisions remain yours.

Public-register information

Where public registers such as the Charity Commission or Companies House are referred to, a dated check records what was available at that time; it is not a guarantee of current status.

Fees and payment

Fees are one-time, payable in advance by card through Stripe, or by Stripe invoice on request. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. No VAT is added to the prices shown.

Refunds

If the setup is not useful, you can ask for a full refund within 60 days of delivery. See the refund and cancellation policy.

Liability

The service organises information you provide. Liability for any claim connected with the service is limited to the fee you paid, except where the law does not allow such a limit.
