Service terms

Terms

These terms describe the basis of the Grant Post-Award Ops founding pilot for business customers.

Business service

Grant Post-Award Ops is a business-to-business administrative support service for grant-making organisations and professional administrators. The founding pilot is a one-time 60-day engagement.

Scope and start of work

The portfolio, deliverables, access arrangements and any exclusions will be agreed in writing before work starts. The pilot covers up to five foundations or grant portfolios unless a different scope is expressly agreed.

Customer responsibilities

The customer is responsible for supplying accurate information, confirming that it is authorised to share that information, and making all grant-making, payment and governance decisions. The customer should review outputs before relying on them.

Administrative support

The service organises records, dates, evidence and exceptions. Information and outputs supplied through the service are administrative support and are not legal, accounting, audit, investment or regulatory advice.

Public-register information

Due-diligence checks may refer to external public registers, including Charity Commission and Companies House records where applicable. External information can change, be delayed, contain errors or become unavailable. A dated check records what was available at the time; it is not a guarantee of current status.

Fees and renewal

The founding pilot costs £490 one-time for 60 days and has no automatic renewal. Any work after the pilot requires a separate written agreement.

Stopping the engagement

The customer can ask to stop the engagement at any time. Refunds are handled under the refund and cancellation policy.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to shopairlind@gmail.com.
